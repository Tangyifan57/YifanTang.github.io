Hello, I’m Yifan Tang (唐逸钒), a Ph.D. student in Energy and Power Engineering at the School of Advanced Energy, Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Fujun Niu.

My research focuses on prelithiation and presodiation strategies for high-energy lithium-ion and sodium-ion batteries. I study how alkali-ion compensation can be integrated with cathode stabilization, interphase engineering, and practical electrode processing, connecting material chemistry with full-cell and pouch-cell performance.

Previously, I received my master’s degree from Ningbo University, advised by Prof. Lei Han and Dr. Guochang Li. My work explored MOF-derived electrodes, single-atom-regulated interfacial chemistry, and metal/2D-semiconductor heterostructures for fast and durable sodium storage.

## Research Interests

<div class="research-interests" markdown="1">

2. Synthesis of high-performance prelithiation/presodiation materials and investigation of their electrochemical reaction mechanisms.

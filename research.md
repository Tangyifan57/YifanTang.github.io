# Research

My work connects **material design, interfacial chemistry, and practical battery performance**. I am particularly interested in how lithium/sodium compensation can improve energy density while supporting stable electrode structures and interfaces.

## Lithium & Sodium Compensation

**Doctoral research · Sun Yat-sen University · Sep. 2025 – Present**

I develop cathode-side and anode-side **prelithiation/presodiation strategies** to compensate for irreversible Li/Na loss. The work examines additive decomposition, ion transport, interphase formation, and electrode structural stability.

A central direction is integrating **alkali-ion compensation with cathode stabilization and interphase engineering**, and evaluating these strategies in full cells and pouch cells with practical electrode processing.

## MOF-Derived Anodes & Interfacial Chemistry

**Master’s research · Ningbo University · Sep. 2022 – Jun. 2025**

I designed **MOF-derived composites, single-atom-regulated electrodes, and metal/2D-semiconductor heterostructures** for sodium-ion batteries. These studies used confinement, interlayer engineering, and interface reconstruction to improve reaction kinetics and structural stability.

The mechanistic work combined **in-situ XRD, X-ray absorption spectroscopy, electron microscopy, and DFT analysis**, focusing on phase evolution, interfacial charge transfer, and Na⁺ adsorption/diffusion. Electrode–electrolyte interfacial chemistry and SEI evolution were further regulated through single-atom catalysis and heterointerface engineering.

Related studies: [Bi/MoSSe heterostructures](https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.76533), [Cu–C₃ sites in Bi@C](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202417602), and [Fe₂Mo₃O₈@C@MoS₂](https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.202403351).

## Earlier Work: Laser Additive Manufacturing

**Bachelor’s thesis · University of South China · Sep. 2020 – Jun. 2022**

I investigated how **TiC reinforcement** affects the solidification microstructure, grain structure, precipitates, and forming defects of laser additively manufactured **CoCrNi medium-entropy alloys**.

## Methods & Tools

- **Electrochemistry:** CV, GCD, EIS, DRT, GITT, PITT, LSV, rate capability, and full-cell/pouch-cell evaluation.
- **Materials characterization:** in-situ XRD and Raman, XAS, XPS, SEM, TEM, FTIR, and in-situ DEMS.
- **Analysis:** Origin, COMSOL, and interpretation of theoretical calculations.
